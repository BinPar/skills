# Post Archetypes

Five archetypes. Every blog piece declares one before drafting. The archetype fixes the word
budget, the section structure and the closing.

| Archetype | What it does | Words | Closing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **marco** | Gives criteria for deciding something | 1.400-1.800 | reflective |
| **mecanismo** | Takes one problem and describes its guarantee | 1.200-1.600 | reflective |
| **sector** | Regulation, adoption, trend | 1.000-1.400 | reflective |
| **evidencia** | How something is measured and what fails | 1.200-1.600 | reflective |
| **nota de producto** | Announces something that shipped | 500-800 | CTA allowed |

**Reflective closing** means the piece returns to the general problem and leaves the reader with a
criterion, not an action. Conversion is the product pages' job. Only the **nota de producto**
takes a CTA, and only one.

Example titles below are listed without the display period they take on the published article.

---

## marco: criteria for deciding something

**When:** the reader has to make a decision and needs a defensible way to make it. Buying,
supervising, accepting, escalating, publishing.

**Thesis shape:** *"Decidir X exige separar A, B y C, y cada una se comprueba distinto."*

**Structure and budget (1.400-1.800 words):**

| Section | Words | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 180-220 | The decision, and why it is usually made badly. No product |
| The distinction | 300-400 | The 2-4 dimensions the piece separates. This is the load-bearing section |
| Criteria per dimension | 500-700 | For each: what to check, and the failure mode it catches. Numbered list is appropriate here |
| Where it breaks | 250-350 | The cases the framework does not cover. Calibrated certainty lives here |
| Closing | 140-180 | What the framework buys the organisation. Reflective |

**Headings:** numbered H2 is acceptable (`## 1. Separar precisión, trazabilidad y utilidad`).

**Opening pattern:**

> "Aceptar una respuesta clínica como fiable es una decisión, no una impresión. En la práctica se
> toma con criterios implícitos: suena razonable, cita algo, llega rápido. Esos criterios no
> resisten una revisión de calidad ni una auditoría interna."

**Closing pattern:**

> "Separar precisión, trazabilidad y utilidad no hace fiable a un sistema. Hace revisable la
> decisión de aceptarlo, que es lo que una organización sanitaria necesita poder sostener."

**Example titles for this archetype:** *"Cómo medir la fiabilidad de una respuesta clínica"*,
*"Qué debe explicar una IA médica antes de recomendar una acción"*, *"Del piloto a producción:
decisiones críticas para IA sanitaria"*.

## mecanismo: a problem and its guarantee

**When:** there is one concrete failure mode worth explaining, and the piece describes what has to
be true for it not to happen. The archetype that carries the disclosure boundary's whole weight.

**Thesis shape:** *"Cuando ocurre X, la cita deja de servir. La garantía es Y."*

**Structure and budget (1.200-1.600 words):**

| Section | Words | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 150-200 | The failure, described concretely. No product, no mechanism |
| Why it happens | 250-350 | The structural cause, at functional level |
| What the guarantee has to cover | 350-450 | The conditions. This is where the product may appear, once |
| What it does not cover | 200-300 | Limits. Non-negotiable in this archetype |
| Closing | 130-160 | The general principle. Reflective |

**Hard rule:** the mechanism may motivate the piece but never appears in it. Use the translation
table in `clinical-red-lines.md` §6 for every sentence that is tempted toward implementation.

**Opening pattern:**

> "Una cita clínica envejece peor que el texto que la sostiene. El documento se actualiza, un
> epígrafe se reordena, un párrafo se reescribe — y la referencia sigue existiendo, apuntando a un
> lugar que ya no dice lo mismo."

**Closing pattern:**

> "Una cita que sobrevive a la actualización del documento no es un detalle técnico. Es la
> diferencia entre una respuesta que puede revisarse y una que solo puede creerse."

**Example titles for this archetype:** *"Del documento fuente a la decisión: evidencia visible en
cada consulta"*, *"Trazabilidad documental como requisito de confianza institucional"*,
*"Diseñar una base de conocimiento sanitario preparada para cambiar"*.

## sector: regulation, adoption, trend

**When:** the subject is the environment, not the product. European regulation, adoption in public
health services, procurement, professional supervision.

**Thesis shape:** *"Lo que está cambiando es X, y la consecuencia operativa para las
organizaciones es Y."*

**Structure and budget (1.000-1.400 words):**

| Section | Words | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 120-170 | What changed, factually. No product |
| What it actually requires | 300-400 | The obligation or the pressure, in operational terms |
| What it means for a clinical organisation | 300-400 | Roles, documents, decisions affected |
| What is still open | 180-250 | Genuine uncertainty. Do not resolve what is unresolved |
| Closing | 100-140 | Reflective |

**Special caution:** this archetype sits closest to red line 3. Analysing the AI Act or MDR is
fine; implying conformity is not. Attribute every regulatory statement to the framework itself,
and avoid dates and article numbers unless verified.

**Opening pattern:**

> "La regulación europea de IA no pregunta si un sistema clínico funciona. Pregunta quién
> supervisa, con qué registro y bajo qué límites — y esa es una pregunta documental antes que
> técnica."

**Example titles for this archetype:** *"Adopción responsable de IA clínica en organizaciones
sanitarias"*, *"Gobernar el conocimiento médico en el sector"*, *"Qué exige un despliegue
responsable de IA en Atención Primaria"*.

## evidencia: how it is measured and what fails

**When:** the subject is measurement itself: benchmarks, control questions, expert review,
metrics, degradation.

**Thesis shape:** *"Medir X con Y no dice lo que parece decir. Lo que hay que observar es Z."*

**Structure and budget (1.200-1.600 words):**

| Section | Words | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 150-200 | The measurement that misleads. No product |
| What the number does say | 250-350 | Fair reading of the usual metric, with its context |
| What it misses | 350-450 | The failure modes no aggregate score catches |
| What to measure instead | 300-400 | Concrete checks, and who runs them |
| Closing | 130-170 | Reflective |

**Hard rule:** this is the archetype where red line 2 is most at risk. Every figure quoted needs
its source and its context, and the piece must distinguish technical signal from clinical
outcome, explicitly, in the text, and not by omission alone.

**Opening pattern:**

> "Un resultado de 600 sobre 600 en un examen de acceso dice algo verificable sobre un sistema y
> nada sobre un paciente. La distancia entre esas dos afirmaciones es donde se decide si una
> evaluación sirve."

**Example titles for this archetype:** *"Evaluar respuestas clínicas sin depender solo de
benchmarks"*, *"Cómo definir preguntas de control para validar conocimiento médico"*,
*"Métricas útiles para observar una plataforma clínica inteligente"*.

## nota de producto

**When:** something shipped and the organisation needs to know it exists.

**The typified exception.** Here the third person of BinPar governs, the product may open the
piece, and one CTA is allowed at the close.

**Structure and budget (500-800 words):**

| Section | Words | Content |
|---|---|---|
| What is available | 100-150 | Plainly. No build-up |
| The problem it addresses | 150-200 | One paragraph of context, operational |
| How it works, functionally | 150-250 | What the roles do, what the organisation sees |
| Limits and prerequisites | 80-120 | What it does not do, what has to be in place |
| CTA | 20-40 | One line, direct |

**Everything else still applies:** no personification, no efficacy claim, no regulatory badge,
functional level only, calibrated certainty, no period on running headings.

**CTA rules:** direct and specific, never generic and never fear-based. Good: *"Revisamos el caso
de uso, las restricciones de seguridad y el nivel de evidencia necesario."* Bad: *"Más
información"*, *"No espere más"*, *"Descubre cómo"*.

**Opening pattern:**

> "BinPar incorpora preguntas de control configurables por la organización. Un supervisor
> define el conjunto de preguntas que deben mantener su respuesta, y cada carga de contenido se
> comprueba contra ellas antes de publicarse."

## Structural conventions for all archetypes

- **H1 = the frontmatter `title`**, matching the filename slug. It renders as the article's
  display headline, so it ends with a period (`language-rules.md` §5); the slug drops it. The
  MDX body does not repeat it: the article page renders the title from the frontmatter.
- **H2/H3 are running headings**: no period.
- **`excerpt`**: one sentence, full stop included, no product name, states the piece's criterion.
  Follow the pattern of the blog's former placeholder excerpts: *"Roles, publicación controlada
  y validación continua para mantener una base clínica auditable."*
- **Heading depth**: H2 for sections, H3 for subsections. H4 only in **marco** and **evidencia**,
  and never more than twice per piece. The seed post goes to H4 (`#### Señales de alerta`)
  which is acceptable there but not a default.
- **One image maximum** per article, after the opening, with a descriptive `alt` that is a
  sentence about what is shown, not a keyword list.
- **One blockquote maximum**, carrying the piece's load-bearing sentence.
- **Lists**: at most two per section, and only for criteria, checks or failure modes.
- **`readTime`** is derived from the word count (210 wpm) and only needs a frontmatter override
  when it reads wrong. Expected per archetype: 1.000-1.200 words ≈ 4-5 min, 1.400-1.600 ≈ 6 min,
  1.800 ≈ 7-8 min.
- **No closing section titled "Conclusión"** in reflective archetypes; the last section carries a
  substantive heading. The seed post uses `## 4. Conclusión`. Do not copy that.
