# Voice and Narrator

> Sources: production copy of the BinPar website (November 2026) and product; the BinPar
> identity manual v1.0 (October 2026); the October 2026 videos; standing positions inherited
> from earlier website pages, owned by this skill.

## 1. Emotional core

One word: **defensibility**.

The reader is a clinician, a systems lead, a quality manager, a medical director or a
procurement committee. They are not looking for a reason to be excited. They are looking for
something they can put in front of a committee, an internal audit or a legal review without
being embarrassed.

The test for any paragraph: **would a medical director sign under it?** If the sentence would
force them to defend a claim they cannot verify, cut it.

The brand's standing position, reusable verbatim wherever the boundary needs stating:

> "El producto se comunica como soporte a decisión y consulta de evidencia, no como sustituto
> del criterio profesional."

Live echo, the website description: *"Plataforma de IA médica institucional para soporte a la
toma de decisiones, evidencia clínica y gobierno del conocimiento sanitario."*

## 2. The four dials in depth

### Certainty: calibrated, never maximal

Declaring limits is a brand asset, not a weakness. A platform whose promise is traceability
cannot write prose that outruns its evidence.

**Calibration vocabulary:** puede enlazar, se diseña para, está previsto, conviene comprobar, en
los casos en que, dentro del corpus disponible, según configuración, salvo que.

**Absolutes to avoid:** siempre, nunca falla, garantiza, elimina el error, asegura la corrección,
cualquier pregunta, todo el conocimiento médico.

| Off-brand | On-brand |
|---|---|
| "Cada respuesta enlaza a su fuente original." | "Cada respuesta puede enlazar al fragmento o página original que sustenta la información clínica." |
| "El sistema garantiza que el conocimiento está actualizado." | "Las actualizaciones se incorporan en entornos controlados, pasan por revisión y se trasladan a producción cuando el equipo responsable lo decide." |

Precision costs words, and the words buy defensibility.

**Commitments vs effects.** In proposals, plans and contracts, what BinPar itself commits to
(scope, deliverables, dates, roles, service levels) is written firmly: *"BinPar entrega el
entorno de preproducción el 15 de enero de 2027."* Hedging a commitment reads as evasion.
Calibration applies to **effects** on clinical practice, which BinPar does not control.

### Concreteness: high

The failure mode is the paragraph that stacks institutional nouns and says nothing checkable:

> "Una capa de IA seria, auditable y preparada para operar dentro de un sistema sanitario."

Nothing false, nothing anchored. **Every claim needs one of four anchors:**

| Anchor | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **A role** | administrador, editor, supervisor, usuario final, comité de calidad, dirección médica |
| **A document** | guía clínica, protocolo de centro, criterio de derivación, versión publicada, epígrafe |
| **A decision** | qué se publica, qué se prioriza, cuándo se pasa a producción, cuándo se deriva |
| **A failure mode** | omitir una contraindicación, citar una fuente que no sostiene la conclusión, responder con más seguridad que la evidencia disponible |

Anchored rewrite:

> "Un editor carga la guía en preproducción, un supervisor clínico la valida contra las preguntas
> de control y solo entonces pasa a producción. Cada respuesta posterior puede enlazar al
> epígrafe concreto que la sustenta."

### Rhythm: medium, not Hemingway

- **One idea per sentence.** Two clauses maximum; the second qualifies the first.
- **Paragraphs of 3-5 lines.** A one-line paragraph is a soundbite.
- **No chained nominalisation.** "la implementación de la validación de la incorporación de
  contenidos" → "los contenidos se validan antes de incorporarse".
- **Verbs over noun phrases.** If the only verb is *ser*, *tener* or *permitir*, rewrite.

Display headlines are the exception to "no soundbites": the manual's two-idea headline is short
on purpose. The entradilla below it carries the anchor.

### Warmth: low but not cold

The brand's affection is respect for professional judgement. It shows up structurally: never in
adjectives about the reader, always in what the system is allowed to do.

**The formula:** *el sistema ordena, el clínico decide.* Model: *"Estos criterios no sustituyen
el juicio clínico. Lo ordenan."*

| Cold | On-brand | Too warm |
|---|---|---|
| "El sistema determina la conducta adecuada." | "El sistema reúne la evidencia; la conducta la decides tú." | "Sabemos lo duro que es tu día a día." |

The conversational voice (§3) puts *tú* and *nosotros* in the sentence; it does not raise this
dial. Warmth still shows in what the reader is left to decide, never in adjectives about them.

## 3. The narrator

### Conversational voice (default)

Since the November 2026 website, BinPar speaks as **nosotros** and addresses the reader as
**tú**: website, blog, evaluator, contact and team pages, videos, social posts, emails and the
product (chat and back office already use *tú* throughout). The reader is still a medical
director or a quality committee. The voice is conversational; the content stays defensible.

**nosotros = the people of BinPar.** It carries actions someone can check: what the team
measured, publishes, delivers or answers.

| On-brand | Off-brand | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Hemos medido la ingesta sobre 24 documentos reales de dos servicios de salud." | "Creemos que la trazabilidad es esencial." | Belief instead of evidence (M5) |
| "Te respondemos en un día laborable." | "Nos apasiona transformar la sanidad." | Feeling plus buzzword (M5, M8) |
| "Publicamos cada versión del corpus con su fecha." | "Somos la IA médica de referencia." | Unverifiable rank (M5, M9) |

**tú = the professional or the person who reads for the organisation.** Use it to hand them a
decision or an action.

| On-brand | Off-brand | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Si tu servicio actualiza el protocolo cada año, la cita tiene que sobrevivir a la actualización." | "Sabemos lo duro que es tu día a día." | Empathy claim, no anchor (M4) |
| "Elige el ámbito y te preguntamos solo lo que cambia el veredicto." | "¿Y si tu equipo pudiera olvidarse de buscar?" | Brochure question (M4) |
| "Abre la cita para ver el pasaje en la página original." | "Confía en la respuesta." | Asks for trust instead of checking (H9) |

Rules:

- **One address per surface: *tú*.** *usted* only in legal text that already uses it and in an
  email thread where the institution writes *usted* (`formats.md` §5). Never both on one
  surface (M4).
- **The system is never *nosotros* and never *yo*.** A chat message that says "hemos revisado
  tu pregunta" or "creo que" personifies the AI (H6). The system keeps documentary verbs in the
  third person: "Respuesta con cuatro fuentes de Nefrología y Cardiología."
- **Imperatives to the reader** are fine for navigation, forms and process ("Solicita una
  reunión", "Completa tus datos", "Abre la cita"). Never for clinical conduct: no sentence tells
  the clinician what to do with a patient (H1, H9).
- **Impersonal sentences stay correct**, and analysis often reads best in them: *"Una respuesta
  puede sonar correcta y omitir una contraindicación."* Conversational means the *nosotros* /
  *tú* frame is available and expected at the seams (openings, transitions, method, calls to
  action), not that every sentence needs it. The two posts published before November 2026 are
  impersonal and remain compliant.
- **Questions:** at most one rhetorical question per piece, answered in the next sentence. A
  landing or tool page may be headed by a question the page answers (*"¿Encaja BinPar en tu
  institución?"*). A blog or document title is never a question.

**Product placement in analytical pieces** (blog, explainer videos, public reports):

| Position | Rule |
|---|---|
| Opening (first 2 paragraphs or first 15 s) | Never. The problem opens the piece |
| Body | At most two mentions, where the product is the natural example |
| Closing | Never as a sale |

The team narrating its own method ("hemos medido", "comparamos dos lecturas") is not
placement; selling the platform is. Test: delete every sentence that sells the platform. If the
piece collapses, it was a brochure.

**Nota de producto** (typified exception): BinPar or *nosotros* may open the piece, one CTA at
the close. Everything else still applies.

### Documents for a known reader who must act

Proposals, client reports, emails, job briefs and internal documents keep the same voice and
add commitment: BinPar (or *nosotros*) is the subject that commits, and the client is named.

| Document | Subject and address | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal, offer, plan | BinPar commits in the third person or as *nosotros*; the client by its name | "BinPar propone un piloto de doce semanas en dos centros de Atención Primaria. El Hospital designa un supervisor clínico por centro." |
| Cover letter signed by a person | First person of the signer, plural for the team | "Adjuntamos la propuesta revisada con los cambios acordados el 3 de noviembre." |
| Email | First person of the sender; *tú*, or *usted* if the institution writes *usted* | "Te escribo para cerrar la fecha de la reunión de arranque." |
| Job brief, technical test, onboarding | *tú* to the candidate or new hire; imperatives in instructions | "Entrega un repositorio con instrucciones para ejecutarlo en local." |
| Internal procedure, minutes, internal report | Team voice, imperatives, internal vocabulary; label *Uso interno* | "Antes de publicar, ejecuta las preguntas de control en preproducción." |

What never relaxes: naming, personification, the red lines, calibrated effects, anchors,
buzzwords, emojis, es-ES formatting. In job briefs and on the team page, also no *rockstar*,
*ninja*, *gurú*, *familia*, *ambiente joven y dinámico*: describe the work, the criteria and the
conditions.

## 4. The name

**BinPar**: one word, capital B and P. The product formerly named *Consensus Salutis* and the
company share one brand.

| Case | Write | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Prose, `alt`, `aria-label`, metadata, email subjects, file titles | "BinPar" | "Binpar", "BINPAR", "Bin Par", "binPar", "Consensus Salutis", "Consensus", "CS" |
| Eyebrow or micro-label in caps | "BinPar" uppercased by CSS or the layout | "BINPAR" typed in the source |
| Name plus qualifier | "BinPar · Lecturas clínicas" | "BinPar - Lecturas clínicas", "BinPar: Inicio" |
| Possessive | "el equipo de BinPar", "Oficinas de BinPar" | "la BinPar", "el BinPar" |
| Legal text | "BinPar Team S.L." | "BinPar S.L.", or the bare name in a legal clause |
| Footer legal line | "© BinPar · Todos los derechos reservados" | English variants |
| Reader who knows the old name | "BinPar (antes Consensus Salutis)", once, at first mention | the old name anywhere else |
| Spoken (voice-over, TTS, events) | «Bínpar»: a *llana* word, stress on the first syllable, BÍN-par | bin-PAR; spelling it out |

`<title>` follows each surface's template: `%s | BinPar` on the website; "BinPar — Chat" (es,
en), "BinPar — Xat" (ca), "BinPar — Editor" in the apps. Do not invent separators.

**Contact data.** Public contact `info@binpar.com`; staff mailboxes `nombre@binpar.com`;
the web address in signatures and print is `binpar.com`, as in the identity manual. The team's
email signature comes from the website's generator (`/mail-generator`), never retyped.

**Addresses and IDs keep their spelling.** Legacy `consensussalutis.com` (and `app.`, `back.`),
mailboxes such as `consensussalutis@binpar.com`, the blog author slug `equipo-consensus-salutis`
(displayed as "Equipo BinPar"). Quote verbatim, never rewrite, never derive a name from them.

## 5. Register notes for es-ES

- **Institutional, not academic.** Avoid *dicho esto*, *cabe señalar que*, *no es óbice*.
- **No anglicisms where Spanish exists:** *insight* → *hallazgo*; *compliance* → *cumplimiento*;
  *pipeline* → *proceso*; *deployment* → *despliegue*; *feature* → *capacidad*; *kick-off* →
  *reunión de arranque*; *deadline* → *fecha límite*.
- **Keep the sector's own terms:** Atención Primaria (capitalised as the level of care), guía
  clínica, protocolo, corpus, trazabilidad, gobierno del dato, SSO, RPO/RTO.
- **Numbers:** thousands with a period (`30.000`), decimals with a comma (`8,5`).
- **Dates:** written out in prose ("12 de octubre de 2026"), as the manual does ("Actualizado el
  12 de octubre de 2026"). Numeric `12/10/2026` only in dense tables.
